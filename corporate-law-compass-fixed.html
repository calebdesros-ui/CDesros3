About

About Corporate Law Compass

A free educational database making landmark corporate legal rulings accessible, searchable, and actionable for business professionals, executives, investors, and students.

Our Mission

Corporate law shapes every significant business decision — from M&A transactions to executive compensation, from hostile takeovers to compliance programs. Yet most of this doctrine lives in dense legal opinions accessible only to specialists.

Corporate Law Compass bridges the gap: every case follows a strict 15-part template that moves from the 30-second overview to a 5–10 minute deep dive, with plain-English definitions, business impact scorecards, and discussion questions designed for classroom and boardroom alike.

The 15-Part Template

Each case entry follows a consistent 15-part structure: Quick Identification · Why This Case Matters · Executive Summary · Timeline · Background · Legal Questions Presented · Court's Decision · Court's Reasoning · Business Impact · Financial Impact · Long-Term Legacy · Key Concepts · Related Cases · Discussion Questions · Sources & Further Reading.
